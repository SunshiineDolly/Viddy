Viddy 0.26.10 improves recommendations and continuous browsing on Windows and Mac.

- Home and Up next now learn from Watch Later, favorites and saved playlists, in addition to follows and recent engaged watch history. Saving the same video in several lists does not multiply its influence.
- Recommendations preserve the actual or inferred video category instead of overwriting it with the search topic. Creator searches rotate across known followed creators, and Home reserves space for unfamiliar subjects before trimming the candidate list. Discover keeps its independent exploration mix.
- Results appear as searches finish. New pages append without rearranging existing cards; failed requests retain the current feed and offer retry.
- Future pages adapt to new saves and follows without restarting the visible feed. Clearing recommendation memory also invalidates mounted feed caches.

Validation includes large synthetic profiles with 5,000 follows and 5,000 history entries, growing Home and Discover feeds, delayed sources, duplicate suppression, failed-page recovery and guest behavior after logout. These checks do not establish subjective recommendation quality or unrestricted provider capacity.

This release does not claim to resolve the remaining Mac YouTube preview problem. Playback diagnostics from the affected Mac are still needed to identify that failure.
