Viddy 0.26.11 keeps your playback queue intact on Windows and Mac while navigating and switching videos.

Known Mac limitation: hover previews are still not confirmed working. The new direct-video preview path passed a controlled clip test, but the real Mac preview test was blocked by YouTube's anonymous-playback check. This release includes that attempted change; it does not claim to resolve the preview problem. A blocked preview displays Preview unavailable. Main video playback is separate from hover previews.

- Mac previews use H.264 video directly, with no embedded YouTube poster or second play button.
- Preview support prepares automatically from checksum-verified official helpers for Apple Silicon and Intel. Homebrew setup is not required for previews. The first launch may take a moment to prepare.
- Leaving a thumbnail stops playback and clears its media source. Preview requests are serialized, canceled before starting when the pointer has left, and cached briefly in memory.
- Failed sources show Preview unavailable and can be retried on a later hover. Preview playback is separate from watch history.
- Windows retains its existing hover player. Account storage, the updater key and the stable Mac signing identity stay unchanged.
- Windows and Mac keep the playback queue when searching, navigating, using Back, opening another video or returning from the pop-out player. The docked queue stays open across video changes. Opening a video outside the queue inserts it after the current item without changing the saved playlist.

The Mac preview diagnostic requires a real HTTPS Google Video source, advancing playback time and decoded frames inside the production thumbnail, muted playback, stopping on leave, the preview preference and restart. A loaded player or provider rejection cannot satisfy the preview check. The diagnostic runs after the signed downloads are prepared, so its result remains visible even when a release with this known limitation is published.
