Viddy 0.26.9 improves Mac playback startup, saved sign-ins, and update notifications on Windows and Mac.

- Connect directly to the embedded player, start selected videos automatically, retry muted previews, and stop stalled preview handshakes.
- Check for updates while Viddy is open and show an Update and restart prompt, with checks every 30 seconds and when returning to the app.
- Migrate older Mac sign-ins into a new protected Keychain entry under the stable signing identity. macOS may ask once to authorize reading an older entry; this update does not bypass system security prompts.
- Add Copy playback diagnostics in Mac Settings → Playback. It includes app versions, public signing status and player connection events, without account credentials or library data.

Verification includes real YouTube playback in a browser, native Mac autoplay and restart checks, and an isolated Keychain migration across two signed app versions. GitHub's Mac runner can be blocked by YouTube bot verification; playback on affected personal Macs still needs confirmation.
